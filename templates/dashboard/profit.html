How this is calculated
  • Each agent is a strategy: on a ▲ Buy day (score 55 or more) you buy at that day's close and hold for {{ hold_days }} day{{ hold_days|pluralize }} (a new Buy or Sell before then replaces it). Days in a row are one trade. {% if short %}On a ▼ Sell day you go short for {{ hold_days }} day{{ hold_days|pluralize }} instead (you gain when the price falls).{% else %}A ▼ Sell means hold nothing for {{ hold_days }} day{{ hold_days|pluralize }}.{% endif %} {% if hold_between %}With no call running you hold Bitcoin: this is how to judge an agent that only sells, like the risk-on day.{% else %}With no call running you hold nothing.{% endif %}
  • Profit is what 100 would have become, minus 100, with every gain reinvested. No fees or slippage. vs holding compares with simply buying on the first day and holding to the last.
  • Worst drop is the largest fall of the account from a high point: the pain on the way. Trades won is the share of trades that ended in profit.
