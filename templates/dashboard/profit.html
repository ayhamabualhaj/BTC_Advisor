How this is calculated
  • Each agent is a strategy: on a ▲ Buy day (score 55 or more) you buy at that day's close and sell at the next day's close. Days in a row are one trade. {% if short %}On a ▼ Sell day you go short instead (you gain when the price falls).{% else %}On Sell and Hold days you hold nothing; the system never shorts.{% endif %}
  • Profit is what 100 would have become, minus 100, with every gain reinvested. No fees or slippage. vs holding compares with simply buying on the first day and holding to the last.
  • Worst drop is the largest fall of the account from a high point: the pain on the way. Trades won is the share of trades that ended in profit.
