How to read this
  • Every agent gives a score from 0 to 100: how sure it is that the price will be higher after {{ horizon }} day{{ horizon|pluralize }}. 55 or more is a ▲ Buy (a score of 70 = “70% sure it rises”), 45 or less a ▼ Sell (a score of 30 = “70% sure it falls”), in between a Hold, which is never judged.
  • A call is right when the price moved its way and wrong when not. “won +3.1%” / “lost −2.0%” is the move seen from the call’s side: a Sell that saw the price fall 3.1% won 3.1%.
  • Hit rate = right ÷ (right + wrong).{% if base_rate %} Always guessing “up” would have been right {{ base_rate|floatformat:0 }}% of the time since 2019 at this horizon, so that is the bar to beat, not 50%.{% endif %}
  • Votes: {{ rule_text }}. The Rules, ML and Opus columns under Vote are the first round; Overall calls only when they all agree. Opus has scores on few days; without them it sits out and Rules and ML must agree. The group lead was chosen on 2019–2024 only.
  • Fair tests: the rules were chosen on Jan 2025 – Sep 2026, so 2019–2024 is new to the rules; the vote was chosen on 2019–2024, so 2025 on is new to the vote; Oct 2026 on is new to everything. ML only ever learns from days whose result was already known, so every ML call is a fair test.
